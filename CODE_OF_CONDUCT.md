# Code of conduct

Xaeo adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct, for everyone taking part in this project: in issues, pull requests, reviews and any other project space.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and experience level, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers at hello@tryxaeo.com. Every report is reviewed and handled in confidence. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may restrict anyone who repeatedly does.
